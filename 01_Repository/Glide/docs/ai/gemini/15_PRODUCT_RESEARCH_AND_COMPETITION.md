# Product research and competition

Stand 19.11.2026 · Glide 1.0.0 · Aufgabenformat 26 · Quellstand 5631333a

## Evidence boundary

The authoritative internal market and inspiration analysis is [`00_Arbeitsvorbereitung/Glide_Markt_und_Vorbilder.md`](../../../../../00_Arbeitsvorbereitung/Glide_Markt_und_Vorbilder.md). It contains dated research, source links, comparisons, and proposals. This document does not refresh external web evidence and should not be used to claim current competitor pricing, features, market share, or user sentiment.

## Product spaces considered

The project has compared task managers and planners, notes/docs workspaces, canvas/board tools, pixel-art applications, local-first systems, and AI-assisted import/export patterns. Each is a reference for specific jobs rather than a claim that Glide replaces every product in the category.

## Documented product principles

- Keep local ownership and offline operation as core constraints.
- Integrate daily planning with structured task data rather than turning the product into a general project-management suite.
- Keep reference pages, notes, board/canvas views, and drawing features connected to a personal workspace.
- Make AI exchange explicit, reviewable, and file-based; Glide itself does not send user data to an AI service.
- Prefer coherent interaction and restrained UI over adding features to match a competitor checklist.

## How to use comparative research

For any comparison or recommendation, record the source and access/research date, the exact product/version/plan observed, which claims are directly evidenced, what remains an inference, and the Glide feature or decision it informs. External screenshots and promotional copy are not independent performance evidence. A competitor's feature does not authorize adding an equivalent to Glide.

## Unresolved research limits

This collection does not reproduce all comparator tables or source excerpts. Refresh the canonical market document before presenting current market claims. The current repository snapshot does not contain a verified independent market-size study or a customer-survey basis for quantified demand; treat such values as UNKNOWN unless the canonical document cites fresh primary sources.
